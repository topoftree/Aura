The ARM64X DLL contains both native ARM64 and ARM64EC views. The i386
DLL, native ntdll, FEX, graphics drivers, and game files are unchanged.

Reproduction: tools/arm64ec/build_system_info.py, package_system_info.py,
system-info/build.json, and docs/WINE_SYSTEM_INFO_SMAPI.md in Aura source.
The builder verifies the pinned kernelbase sources and retains the base
runtime's Android adaptations. Host instruction replay does not establish
that SMAPI or a game has run successfully on an Android device.
