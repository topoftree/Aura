Aura Ground Zeroes GetTickCount entry adapter

Based on Wine / Proton Wine 11.0-2 at
https://github.com/The412Banner/proton-wine/tree/dcc50c55e2a4dbd06610e5b45d8872bc6f048633
with its pinned Android ARM64EC vendor patches and Aura wait patches.
Wine and its contributors retain their copyright. The new helper and loader
integration are by Aura contributors, 2026, under LGPL-2.1-or-later.
See COPYING.LIB, wine-mgs-tick-thunk.patch and aura_mgs_tick_thunk.h.
Rebuild instructions and pinned inputs: tools/arm64ec/build_mgs_tick_thunk.py
and tools/arm64ec/mgs-tick-thunk/build.json in the matching source tree.

Opt-in only: AURA_MGS_TICK_THUNK=1 and main executable MgsGroundZeroes.exe.
Only builtin kernel32!GetTickCount export resolution is adapted. Its clock
implementation and results are unchanged; no game file is modified or included.
This is a compatibility candidate; Android gameplay verification is pending.
