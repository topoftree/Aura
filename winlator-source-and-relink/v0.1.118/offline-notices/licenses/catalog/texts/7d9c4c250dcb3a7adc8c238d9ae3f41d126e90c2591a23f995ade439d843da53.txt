This adapter selects en-US or zh-CN for the Windows user/system/UI language
without changing Android Bionic's UTF-8 Unix codepage or numeric formatting.

Base source and prior Android/wait/stack changes: tools/arm64ec/wait-completion/README.md.
Additional patch and reproducible build/packaging: tools/arm64ec/android-locale/README.md.
